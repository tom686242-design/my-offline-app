Villager Breeder

1. Place two villagers in a 9x9 farm area.
2. Add 3 beds per villager.
3. Toss them 12 carrots each.

Mob Farm

Build a 20-block high tower with a 2x2 drop chute to bring mobs to 1 HP.

How to start a fire
